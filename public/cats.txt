Room IV. The Cattery.
Not on the map. Not in the nav. Hung here for whoever reads the plain files.

The Greeks had a word for the animal before they had the animal:
αἴλουρος, ailouros, "the one with the waving tail." Herodotus saw them
in Egypt and wrote it down (II.66). Nobody has improved on the word.
