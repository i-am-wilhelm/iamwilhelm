Self-hosted webfonts, latin subsets, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

- Cormorant Garamond (300, 400, 500, 600; 400, 500 and 600 italic; the 500 italic is for the Seen pages mounted from their source files, src/lib/seen.ts): display and the gallery pieces. From @fontsource/cormorant-garamond.
- EB Garamond (variable, normal and italic): text. From @fontsource-variable/eb-garamond.
- EB Garamond, greek and greek-ext subsets (variable, normal): the Greek in the gallery essays (Poem.astro declares them as 'EB Garamond Greek' with unicode-range). Same package.
- JetBrains Mono (variable): catalog numbers. Copied from advancing-liberty-media2.
- Press Start 2P, Silkscreen: the ported ALM console and cartridges (Room III). Copied from advancing-liberty-media2.
- Bodoni Moda (variable, wght + opsz; latin, latin-ext, math; normal and italic): True Amazons display. The math subset carries the Greek letters its display Greek is set in, as Google Fonts served the original artifact. From @fontsource-variable/bodoni-moda.
- Literata (variable, wght + opsz; latin, latin-ext, greek, greek-ext; normal and italic): True Amazons text, including its Greek. From @fontsource-variable/literata.
- JetBrains Mono (variable, wght; latin, latin-ext, greek), declared as 'Amazons Mono': True Amazons labels, which set Greek in mono (the site's jetbrains-mono-var.woff2 has no Greek). From @fontsource-variable/jetbrains-mono.
- Big Shoulders Stencil Display (variable, wght; latin), Newsreader (variable, wght + opsz; latin; normal and italic), Courier Prime (400, 700; latin): Everyone, Searchable and Everyone, Serialized, declared under the family names their prototypes load from Google Fonts, with the same unicode-ranges, so each page renders exactly as its prototype did. From @fontsource-variable/big-shoulders-stencil-display, @fontsource-variable/newsreader and @fontsource/courier-prime.
- Before and Behind (In the Original No. 5) adds no files: it declares Cormorant Garamond 500 (normal and italic) from the files above for its text, and the JetBrains Mono greek file as 'Limen Mono' for the θ in its one equation.
- Where It Was reuses the True Amazons set above (Bodoni Moda, Literata with greek and greek-ext for its polytonic Greek, and the JetBrains Mono greek/latin files declared as 'Lens Mono'); no files of its own.
- Martian Mono (variable, wght; latin, latin-ext) and GFS Didot (400; latin, greek, greek-ext): Mimics & Mythmakers instruments and its Greek zone names, declared under their Google Fonts names with the same unicode-ranges, alongside the Bodoni Moda and Literata files above. Questions Wearing Teeth (Mythmaking No. 2) declares the same four families the same way; no files of its own. From @fontsource-variable/martian-mono and @fontsource/gfs-didot.
- IBM Plex Mono (400, 500; latin): the Seen essays (The Double Image, Varo's Workshop, Monet Invented the Dither), whose equations, corner labels and glyph fields are set in it, declared under its Google Fonts name with the same unicode-range. The Greek letters of the glyph ramp fall through to the system monospace, as they did in the prototypes. Their Cormorant Garamond and Newsreader are the files above. From @fontsource/ibm-plex-mono.
- Big Shoulders Display (variable, wght; latin), Public Sans (variable, wght; latin), Black Ops One (400; latin), Doto (variable, wght; latin) and IBM Plex Mono 600 (latin): Face Forward and The Receipts, mounted from their source files (src/lib/futurist.ts) and declared under the family names, weights and unicode-range those files ask Google Fonts for. These five files are the latin subsets exactly as fonts.gstatic.com serves them. The Receipts' IBM Plex Mono 400 and 500 are the files above.
- Barlow Condensed (600, 800; latin) and DM Mono (400, 500; latin): Depth Chart, mounted from its source file (src/lib/futurist.ts) and declared under the family names, weights and unicode-range that file asks Google Fonts for. These four files are the latin subsets exactly as fonts.gstatic.com serves them. Its Public Sans is the file above (the same bytes Google serves).
- Caprasimo (400; latin), Caveat (700; latin) and Courier Prime italic (400; latin): Per My Last Memo, mounted from its source file (src/lib/seen.ts) and declared under the family names, weights and unicode-range the file asks Google Fonts for, since its canvases set ctx.font by family name. These three files are the latin subsets exactly as fonts.gstatic.com serves them; its Courier Prime 400 and 700 and its Newsreader are the files above (the Courier Prime pair is byte-identical to Google's).
- IBM Plex Sans (variable, wght; latin), Instrument Serif (400 and 400 italic; latin), Libre Caslon Display (400; latin), Libre Caslon Text (400, 700 and 400 italic; latin) and Libre Franklin (variable, wght; latin; normal and italic): Fall Line and Off Soundings, mounted from their source files (src/lib/seen.ts) and declared under the family names, weights and unicode-range those files ask Google Fonts for, since their canvases set ctx.font by family name. - Alegreya (variable, wght; latin normal and italic, greek and greek-ext normal): Free Won't (Futurist No. 7), mounted from its source file (src/lib/futurist.ts) and declared under the family name, weight range and unicode-ranges the file asks Google Fonts for, since its canvas sets ctx.font by family name.
