Self-hosted webfonts, latin subsets, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

- Cormorant Garamond (300, 400, 500, 600; 400 and 600 italic): display and the gallery pieces. From @fontsource/cormorant-garamond.
- EB Garamond (variable, normal and italic): text. From @fontsource-variable/eb-garamond.
- EB Garamond, greek and greek-ext subsets (variable, normal): the Greek in the gallery essays (Poem.astro declares them as 'EB Garamond Greek' with unicode-range). Same package.
- JetBrains Mono (variable): catalog numbers. Copied from advancing-liberty-media2.
- Press Start 2P, Silkscreen: the ported ALM console and cartridges (Room III). Copied from advancing-liberty-media2.
- Bodoni Moda (variable, wght + opsz; latin, latin-ext, math; normal and italic): True Amazons display. The math subset carries the Greek letters its display Greek is set in, as Google Fonts served the original artifact. From @fontsource-variable/bodoni-moda.
- Literata (variable, wght + opsz; latin, latin-ext, greek, greek-ext; normal and italic): True Amazons text, including its Greek. From @fontsource-variable/literata.
- JetBrains Mono (variable, wght; latin, latin-ext, greek), declared as 'Amazons Mono': True Amazons labels, which set Greek in mono (the site's jetbrains-mono-var.woff2 has no Greek). From @fontsource-variable/jetbrains-mono.
- Big Shoulders Stencil Display (variable, wght; latin), Newsreader (variable, wght + opsz; latin; normal and italic), Courier Prime (400, 700; latin): Everyone, Searchable, declared under the family names its prototype loads from Google Fonts, with the same unicode-ranges, so the page renders exactly as the prototype did. From @fontsource-variable/big-shoulders-stencil-display, @fontsource-variable/newsreader and @fontsource/courier-prime.
- Where It Was reuses the True Amazons set above (Bodoni Moda, Literata with greek and greek-ext for its polytonic Greek, and the JetBrains Mono greek/latin files declared as 'Lens Mono'); no files of its own.
- Martian Mono (variable, wght; latin, latin-ext) and GFS Didot (400; latin, greek, greek-ext): Mimics & Mythmakers instruments and its Greek zone names, declared under their Google Fonts names with the same unicode-ranges, alongside the Bodoni Moda and Literata files above. From @fontsource-variable/martian-mono and @fontsource/gfs-didot.
