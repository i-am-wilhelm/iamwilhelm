Everyone, Searchable

By Michael WilhelmNovember 2026

Everyone,Searchable

Every human life is about to become searchable through a god-level AI. It will break the searcher before it breaks the searched, and the one move that holds is to get known before you get searched.

That which ye have spoken in the ear in closets shall be proclaimed upon the housetops.

Luke 12:3

I

The Index

Chapter I of IX

Everyone is about to become searchable. Type a name, get a life.

Every text, email, photo, DM and deleted post since the first digital message. The viewing logs your smart TV ships home. The audio your phone catches. Every location ping. All of it compiled and cross-referenced.

Out of all that comes a simulacrum of each of us, a working model of you. Anyone can query it through an AI assistant that will feel, to most people, like a god.

Ask it anything about anyone. Where they are. What they're into. Their alter egos and burner accounts. Everything once thought private gets aired out on request.

People will lose their minds.

II

The Polyneural

Chapter II of IX

Then the connections. Who you talk to, how often, and at what hour.

And who you suddenly stopped talking to.

Call it the Polyneural, a living map of every relationship on earth. Three hops out, everyone connects to a predator, a fraudster or a scandal, and guilt by association goes universal.

III

Already Here

Chapter III of IX

We thought digital privacy was a blackout curtain. It was sheer the whole time. The data is already archived, and the machinery is already here.

2015

Ashley Madison is breached and 32 million accounts spill. Five years later the extortion emails are still landing.

2020

Microsoft is granted a patent for building a chatbot of a specific person out of their posts, messages, voice and photos. Living or dead.

2024

Two Harvard students wire Meta's camera glasses to a face-search engine and an AI, then pull strangers' names and addresses off the street in real time.

2025 · 2026

Texas sues five TV makers, alleging their sets capture what plays on screen and sell it. Then Meta is reported to be planning facial recognition for its glasses, internally called Name Tag.

Who builds the rest? A government, more likely a corporation. Or nobody at all. Millions of AI agents run small lookups until the index assembles itself, an immaculate conception if you trust the official narrative.

Watch for the blur around the few it protects.

IV

The Searcher

Chapter IV of IX

Start with what it does to the person searching. The first search always has a good reason.

Then curiosity. Then suspicion. Then jealousy. Then leverage.

Then fantasy, because this thing retrieves and it also performs. Ask it to play your ex, your crush, your rival. People will rehearse arguments with puppets, then resent the real person for never saying the puppet's lines.

Every query you run lands in your own file. The voyeur's record is the most damning one in the archive.

V

The Genie

Chapter V of IX

The genie only grants wishes as big as the wisher's language. In 2017, 19 in 100 U.S. adults scored at the lowest literacy levels.

By 2023 it was 28 in 100.

Language is the operating system. Hand god-level power to people who can only form small, mean questions and you get small, mean answers at god-level scale.

VI

Reveal Day

Chapter VI of IX

Picture it arriving the way a leak arrives, all at once. The truest black swan humanity has ever faced together.

We've had a rehearsal. On January 2, 1992, Germany opened the Stasi files to the people they were written about.

Vera Wollenberger read hers and learned that the informant code-named Donald was her own husband.

Now picture everyone holding their own file, and everyone else's. Relationships end in an instant. Relations change shape overnight.

The cruelest part is the timing. People normally face themselves in private first. The index collapses the private reckoning and the public one into one instant.

Search yourself, and you find what you refused to face at the exact moment everyone else does.

VII

Who Comes Through

Chapter VII of IX

How it goes for any one person comes down to two things. How fully they've accepted themselves, and how accountable they've been to the people they've affected.

The Integrated already did the work. Their people heard it from them first, and they come through bruised and whole.

The Shameless barely get dented. The Penitent made amends to everyone but themselves. The Divided avoided the mirror and the apology, and they get hit first and hardest.

One group sits off the chart entirely, the people whose hiding keeps them alive. The woman who fled an abuser. The kid in a hostile house. The dissident. The index can't tell a hypocrite from someone in hiding.

VIII

The Come-to-Jesus Move

Chapter VIII of IX

Look at the god being built. An intelligence that knows every sin and forgives none. Scripture has a name for that figure. The accuser.

The God of the come-to-Jesus moment does the opposite. “I will remember their sin no more.” Mercy as chosen forgetting.

In John 8, the accusers walk away one by one, “beginning at the eldest.” They had the longest files.

So when you're handed “damned if you use it, damned if you don't,” walk the third way.

Get known before you get searched.

Seven movesBefore Reveal Day

  1. 01

    Search yourself first, while your reckoning can still be private.

  2. 02

    Tell your people first. Every secret has two possible narrators, you or a machine talking to a stranger. Be the first one.

  3. 03

    Be one person. The index feeds on the double life, and a single life starves it.

  4. 04

    Walk backward with the blanket. When you could look, love sometimes means leaving the file closed.

  5. 05

    Drop your stone. The index runs on our hunger to condemn. Starve it.

  6. 06

    Build the mirror both ways. If someone searches you, you see it. Your data is your property. In 1791, the same year Bentham published his Panopticon, America ratified the Fourth Amendment. Our papers live on servers now, and the principle holds.

  7. 07

    Guard your rough-draft room. Read. Build your vocabulary. Raise kids who can imagine better wishes than the autocomplete.

IX

Fear Not

Chapter IX of IX

Luke 12 says what's whispered in closets will be shouted from the housetops. A few verses later: “the very hairs of your head are all numbered. Fear not.”

Total knowledge, paired with total worth. The index will number every hair on your head with all the love of a spreadsheet. The love part was always our job.

Meet your neighbors now, before things get cataclysmic.

This is the age of Aquarius. The vessel tips, and everything it ever held comes pouring out.

Get known before you get searched.
