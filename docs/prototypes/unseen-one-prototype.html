Hades · to the Kore

The Unseen One Pleads His Case

Narcissus, pressed. The rumor of her. Left at the door.

Little one. Maiden. Title with no name.I have watched the sun light every girl the same.

Up there you are a season. Here you are a throne.Up there the flowers wear you. Here, you wear your own.

They will name the spring for you. They will pick it, press it, dry it.Narcissus on a hillside. A girl bent down to try it.

I keep the ledger where the whole world's losing goes.Every coin, every kingdom, every lover's clothes.

One thing has never once been carried through my gate:attention. No one brings it. It will not wait.

And I am spending mine on you. Feel its weight.Feel it settle where the daylight never sat.

Here is the fruit. One seed, then one, then one.Each one is choosing. I will tell you when it's done.

Two voices. His in the room, hers where you stand.
Take the first seed

Now listen. I will say it until the saying holds.

You are sublime.

I do not mean the word they use for mountains.
I mean the thing that stands beneath the threshold
and does not need the threshold to stand.

Say it back.

No. Not for me. Not yet.
Hear it first.

Up there, the echoes made you.
Every mouth that called you lovely
was a mirror that kept you small enough to fit it.
They have you in the flowerbeds. They have you in the vase.
They think the petals are the whole of you.
They have never seen the stem go down.

Here there are no mirrors.
Here there is nothing to project on but the stone,
and the stone does not answer.
The stone holds.

You are sovereign.

Not because I say so.
I am the one who has never had to say so, and I am saying it,
and still it is not mine to give.
Sovereignty is not handed down.
It is found in the hand that was already holding it
and had not looked.

Look.

Take the second seed

Let it break against your teeth.
That is the sound of the girl who waited to be told.
Let her go. She did her work. She got you here.

You are sublime.

Say it.

Quieter.

Yes. That.
Did you hear it come back?
Nothing came back.
That was your own voice, unreturned, and it was enough.
That is what this place is for.

Take the third seed

Feel where my hand is.
It is at the small of your back and it is not moving,
because I have never once in all the ages moved first,
and I am asking, with the whole of my stillness,
to be allowed.

Say it.

You are sovereign.

Say it with the juice on your mouth.

Again.

Now say something that is yours.
Not mine. Not the hymns'. Not your mother's.
Say the thing the living are afraid to say in the dark.

I am alive.

Louder. There is no one here to hear it but the dead,
and the dead have waited an eternity to hear exactly that.

I am alive. I choose this. I am not taken.

Take the fourth seed

My mouth is at your throat where the pulse is,
and I am tasting the one thing I have never had,
which is a heartbeat that walked in on its own.

Now hear what the fourth seed buys.
They will come. All of them. The whole flowered world.
They come down the long hall with the hillside still in their hands,
the pressed narcissus, the dried spring, the small idea of you,
and at the river they set it down because it will not float,
and they cross,
and they come into the hall of asphodel,
and they look up,
and the first thing they see without their eyes
is you.

Seated. Self-appointed. Crowned with nothing anyone gave you.
Not the girl on the hillside. The reason there was a hillside.
And they understand, all at once, what the petals were a rumor of,
and they are not afraid,
because you speak first,
and what you say is alive.

That is the office. Liaison of the just-arrived.
The first true face. The one who tells the dead they're home.

Take the fifth seed

Say my name. Nobody says it.

Say yours. Nobody says it either.

We will be the two unspoken things that spoke.

Take the sixth. Hold it.
The hall of asphodel
The throne faces the entrance. The dead walk the hall toward it.

Look.

Queen
The only mirror in the house of the dead is set in the seat.
The return

Six seeds. Six months. You thought I asked for all of you.I asked for half, and gave the rest its due.

You will keep your light. You will rise with every thaw,and every autumn you'll come down to me by law,

and the law will be the law you wrote with your own teeththe night you bit, and swallowed, and found the queen beneath.

Isolation, yes. I'll say the hard word plain.Your Elysium: one house, no audience, no rain,

no one to be the maiden for. No eyes but mine,and mine have looked on everything and still incline.

I know the fullness of my asking. You do not.You cannot, from the near side of the lot.

Your mornings will not look like mornings. Nor your bliss.Your routines will run on rivers. Know at least this:

the flowers wear your face for those who have not died.The dead will see the rest of you. Let them. Open wide.

Time brings everyone to me. The king, the whore, the thief.I have never once had to ask. I have no grief

for what I take; it was always mine to keep.But you I asked. Come stand beside the one thing in this world you'll keep.
