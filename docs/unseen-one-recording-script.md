# The Unseen One Pleads His Case — recording script

Two voices. HIM is Hades: low, slow, never hurried, never raised; a king who has never once had to ask, asking. HER is the Kore becoming Persephone: she speaks only her own lines, and only the ones she is told to say. Her first lines are small. Her last ones are not.

Room: dead. No reverb on him; the page adds none either. Her lines should sound close, as if spoken from where the listener is standing.

Pauses are part of the score. "Hold" is dead air the actor leaves at the end of the take, in seconds. Do not fill it.

Each cue becomes one audio file named by its cue id (`U-02-03.mp3`). One take per cue, no breath across cue boundaries.

Cue ids: U-<part>-<line>. Parts: 00 couplets, 01–05 litany, 06 metamorphosis, 07 Elysium, 08 return.

## Part 00 — the couplets (HIM, read straight, hold 1.5 after each couplet)

- U-00-01 — Little one. Maiden. Title with no name. / I have watched the sun light every girl the same.
- U-00-02 — Up there you are a season. Here you are a throne. / Up there the flowers wear you. Here, you wear your own.
- U-00-03 — They will name the spring for you. They will pick it, press it, dry it. / Narcissus on a hillside. A girl bent down to try it.
- U-00-04 — I keep the ledger where the whole world's losing goes. / Every coin, every kingdom, every lover's clothes.
- U-00-05 — One thing has never once been carried through my gate: / attention. No one brings it. It will not wait.
- U-00-06 — And I am spending mine on you. Feel its weight. / Feel it settle where the daylight never sat.
- U-00-07 — Here is the fruit. One seed, then one, then one. / Each one is choosing. I will tell you when it's done. (hold 2.5)

## Part 01 — first seed (HIM unless marked)

- U-01-01 — Now listen. I will say it until the saying holds. (hold 1.4)
- U-01-02 — You are sublime. (hold 1.2)
- U-01-03 — I do not mean the word they use for mountains. I mean the thing that stands beneath the threshold and does not need the threshold to stand. (hold 0.8)
- U-01-04 — Say it back. (hold 2.2 — she does not answer)
- U-01-05 — No. Not for me. Not yet. Hear it first. (hold 1.0)
- U-01-06 — Up there, the echoes made you. Every mouth that called you lovely was a mirror that kept you small enough to fit it. They have you in the flowerbeds. They have you in the vase. They think the petals are the whole of you. They have never seen the stem go down. (hold 1.0)
- U-01-07 — Here there are no mirrors. Here there is nothing to project on but the stone, and the stone does not answer. The stone holds. (hold 1.2)
- U-01-08 — You are sovereign. (hold 1.2)
- U-01-09 — Not because I say so. I am the one who has never had to say so, and I am saying it, and still it is not mine to give. Sovereignty is not handed down. It is found in the hand that was already holding it and had not looked. (hold 1.0)
- U-01-10 — Look. (hold 2.2)
- U-01-11 — Take the second seed. (hold 0.9) [plays when the second seed is taken]

## Part 02 — second seed

- U-02-01 — Let it break against your teeth. That is the sound of the girl who waited to be told. Let her go. She did her work. She got you here. (hold 1.0)
- U-02-02 — You are sublime. (hold 1.2)
- U-02-03 — Say it. (hold 2.2)
- U-02-04 — HER — You are sublime. (small, almost not said; hold 1.0)
- U-02-05 — Quieter. (hold 1.8)
- U-02-06 — HER — You are sublime. (quieter than the first; hold 1.4)
- U-02-07 — Yes. That. Did you hear it come back? Nothing came back. That was your own voice, unreturned, and it was enough. That is what this place is for. (hold 1.2)
- U-02-08 — Take the third seed. (hold 0.9)

## Part 03 — third seed

- U-03-01 — Feel where my hand is. It is at the small of your back and it is not moving, because I have never once in all the ages moved first, and I am asking, with the whole of my stillness, to be allowed. (hold 1.4)
- U-03-02 — Say it. (hold 2.2)
- U-03-03 — HER — You are sovereign. (hold 1.0)
- U-03-04 — Say it with the juice on your mouth. (hold 1.4)
- U-03-05 — HER — You are sovereign. (steadier; hold 1.0)
- U-03-06 — Again. (hold 2.2)
- U-03-07 — HER — You are sovereign. (hold 1.4)
- U-03-08 — Now say something that is yours. Not mine. Not the hymns'. Not your mother's. Say the thing the living are afraid to say in the dark. (hold 2.0)
- U-03-09 — HER — I am alive. (hold 1.2)
- U-03-10 — Louder. There is no one here to hear it but the dead, and the dead have waited an eternity to hear exactly that. (hold 1.4)
- U-03-11 — HER — I am alive. I choose this. I am not taken. (full voice, no tremor; hold 2.0)
- U-03-12 — Take the fourth seed. (hold 0.9)

## Part 04 — fourth seed

- U-04-01 — My mouth is at your throat where the pulse is, and I am tasting the one thing I have never had, which is a heartbeat that walked in on its own. (hold 1.4)
- U-04-02 — Now hear what the fourth seed buys. They will come. All of them. The whole flowered world. They come down the long hall with the hillside still in their hands, the pressed narcissus, the dried spring, the small idea of you, and at the river they set it down because it will not float, and they cross, and they come into the hall of asphodel, and they look up, and the first thing they see without their eyes is you. (hold 1.4)
- U-04-03 — Seated. Self-appointed. Crowned with nothing anyone gave you. Not the girl on the hillside. The reason there was a hillside. And they understand, all at once, what the petals were a rumor of, and they are not afraid, because you speak first, and what you say is alive. (hold 1.2)
- U-04-04 — That is the office. Liaison of the just-arrived. The first true face. The one who tells the dead they're home. (hold 1.6)
- U-04-05 — Take the fifth seed. (hold 0.9)

## Part 05 — fifth seed (the names)

- U-05-01 — Say my name. Nobody says it. (hold 2.4)
- U-05-02 — HER — Hades. (said plainly, like a fact; hold 1.6)
- U-05-03 — Say yours. Nobody says it either. (hold 2.4)
- U-05-04 — HER — Persephone. (first time she has ever said it; hold 2.0)
- U-05-05 — We will be the two unspoken things that spoke. (hold 1.6)
- U-05-06 — Take the sixth. (hold 0.9)
- U-05-07 — Hold it. (hold 1.6)
- U-05-08 — Hold it. (hold 1.8) [loops while the viewer holds the seed]
- U-05-09 — Now. (hold 1.2) [plays on release]

## Part 06 — the metamorphosis (HIM except the word; cadence accelerates, lines shorten)

- U-06-01 — Now it is in you and it is working. (hold 1.4)
- U-06-02 — Feel it go down. (hold 1.2)
- U-06-03 — Feel the hillside leave your hands. (hold 0.6)
- U-06-04 — Feel the hands. (hold 0.6)
- U-06-05 — Feel them stop being a girl's hands (no hold)
- U-06-06 — and start being hands that have held a verdict. (hold 1.2)
- U-06-07 — Your skin is doing what the seed does under ground. (hold 0.8)
- U-06-08 — Splitting. (hold 1.4)
- U-06-09 — Not breaking. Splitting. (hold 0.6)
- U-06-10 — The way a thing splits that was always going to be two things (no hold)
- U-06-11 — and was only waiting for the dark to be allowed. (hold 1.4)
- U-06-12 — I am all the way against you now. (hold 0.8)
- U-06-13 — I have never been all the way against anything. (hold 0.8)
- U-06-14 — I am the unseen and you are seeing me, (no hold)
- U-06-15 — and I am undone by it, and I am not ashamed, (no hold)
- U-06-16 — and you are not a maiden, (hold 0.4)
- U-06-17 — you are not a maiden, (hold 0.4, second time lower and closer)
- U-06-18 — say what you are— (cut off; hold 1.6)
- U-06-19 — HER — Queen. (hold 1.4)
- U-06-20 — Again. With my weight on it. (hold 1.2)
- U-06-21 — HER — Queen. (the whole chest behind it; hold 2.0)
- U-06-22 — The flowers up there are turning in their beds. They feel it. They don't know what they feel. A whole spring shuddering because something underneath it just took its own name in its own mouth with a king's mouth on it. (hold 1.4)
- U-06-23 — Your throat, where I was. Your back, where my hand was and is and will be. Your voice, that came back with nothing on it, coming back now with everything, the whole dark hall ringing with a sovereign in it. (hold 1.6)
- U-06-24 — There. That. (hold 1.6)
- U-06-25 — That was the girl going. That was the stem going down. That was the throne arriving in the body that was already sitting on it. (hold 1.6)
- U-06-26 — Breathe. (hold 2.4)
- U-06-27 — You are the only living thing in the house of the dead, and I am the only thing in the house of the dead that is on its knees. (hold 1.8)
- U-06-28 — Rise. (hold 3.0)

## Part 07 — Elysium

- U-07-01 — Look. (hold 2.4) [plays as the mirror appears]

## Part 08 — the return (HIM, couplets, hold 1.5 after each; the voice has light in it again)

- U-08-01 — Six seeds. Six months. You thought I asked for all of you. / I asked for half, and gave the rest its due.
- U-08-02 — You will keep your light. You will rise with every thaw, / and every autumn you'll come down to me by law,
- U-08-03 — and the law will be the law you wrote with your own teeth / the night you bit, and swallowed, and found the queen beneath.
- U-08-04 — Isolation, yes. I'll say the hard word plain. / Your Elysium: one house, no audience, no rain,
- U-08-05 — no one to be the maiden for. No eyes but mine, / and mine have looked on everything and still incline.
- U-08-06 — I know the fullness of my asking. You do not. / You cannot, from the near side of the lot.
- U-08-07 — Your mornings will not look like mornings. Nor your bliss. / Your routines will run on rivers. Know at least this:
- U-08-08 — the flowers wear your face for those who have not died. / The dead will see the rest of you. Let them. Open wide.
- U-08-09 — Time brings everyone to me. The king, the whore, the thief. / I have never once had to ask. I have no grief
- U-08-10 — for what I take; it was always mine to keep. / But you I asked. Come stand beside the one thing in this world you'll keep. (hold 4.0, then silence)

## Notes for the session

- Her name cues (U-05-02, U-05-04) are the only lines in the piece not printed on the page. On the page the names are unsaid; in the room they are said. Record them.
- HIM never gets louder across the whole piece. Intensity comes from getting slower and closer.
- HER has nine cues. Her arc is: not said (01), barely (02-04), barely (02-06), steady (03-03 to 03-07), alive (03-09, 03-11), the names (05-02, 05-04), the word (06-19, 06-21). Record in order so the arc lives in the voice.
- Deliver as 48k/24-bit WAV masters plus mp3; the site uses mp3 under `public/audio/unseen/`.
