First, where a name usually goes.

You signed one petition. In most operations that signature now lives in nine places: donations, email, the CRM, texting, calling, canvassing, the website, events, and the treasurer's spreadsheet.

None of them talk to each other.

So the copies drift. One has your new address. One still has the old one. One marked you unsubscribed. One never heard.
