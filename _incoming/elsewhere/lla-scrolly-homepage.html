How 900 happens

Liberty spreads the way fire does: one mind at a time.

Nobody recruits nine hundred people at once. One person decides to run. Someone who watched them decides next. The Academy makes sure each of them knows what to do the morning after they decide.

Apply. Learn. Lead.

C. Michael Pickens

Founder and Lead Instructor

Michael has run for office, managed campaigns, and built local organizations from scratch, and he has trained candidates, staff and organizers for more than a decade. He wrote Libertarian Leadership and The Liberated Mind. He teaches the way he coaches: direct, practical, and about what you will do next week.
