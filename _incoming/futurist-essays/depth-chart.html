A core sample of where your money sits when the dollar takes a hit.

Something is inbound. Sound money people have tracked it for 55 years. This log drills straight down through the five places a saver can stand when it lands. At each depth it reads off three things: what the money is priced in, whose promise holds it up, and who can switch it off.

GROUND SURFACE

01 · Incoming

The meteor has been falling since 1971

88%

88% of the dollar's 1971 buying power is gone. What $1.00 bought in the year the gold window closed costs $8.25 today, by the government's own price index.1

That is the slow version, and it is policy. The Fed's own 2% target takes 18% off a dollar every ten years.

The fast version is the one people argue about: a break in confidence that does in a year what inflation did in fifty. Most economists rate a full collapse as unlikely, and one review of reserve-currency history turned up long slides with no sudden collapse among them.2 Others say the break is close.

Slow or fast, the question for a saver is the same. Where are you standing?

02 · The rig

The new rails are built. Read what they carry.

Two claims travel together online. One: central banks and the well informed are moving value to places a falling dollar cannot reach. Two: stablecoins are that place. The record backs the first. The second runs into the fine print of the law.

Two bills get named as the infrastructure. One of them is finished.

  1. 18 JUL 2025The GENIUS Act is signed. It is the first federal law for dollar stablecoins.3
  2. 15 SEP 2026The CLARITY Act, the wider market-structure bill, fails a Senate vote 49 to 50. It is stalled.4
  3. 3 NOV 2026The midterms flip both chambers of Congress. The new members sit on January 3.4
  4. 18 JAN 2027GENIUS takes effect by this date at the latest.3

Here is what GENIUS requires of anyone who issues a dollar stablecoin in the United States.

Reserves
One for one, in cash and short-term Treasuries.3
Interest
The issuer may pay the holder none.5
Off switch
No license without the built-in ability to freeze, seize or burn tokens on a lawful order.3

The rails carry the dollar. A crash that herds people onto stablecoins herds them onto a faster dollar with a switch on it.

06 · The play

The play is knowing your depth

Tokenization puts all five layers on one screen. A stablecoin, a Treasury token and a gold token look alike in a wallet. They sit at very different depths, and most of what is tokenized today sits above the line: roughly three dollars of government debt for every dollar of gold.1214

Three questions sort any place you keep value.

  1. What is it priced in?Above the dollar line or below it.
  2. Whose promise is it?Read the chain. Count the links.
  3. Who holds the off switch?A bank, an issuer, a gatekeeper, a vault, or you.

Depth has a price. Each layer down trades the dollar's slow leak for something else: a market that swings, a vault you have to trust, a key you can lose, a sale that takes time. Zero promises also means zero help.

Know your depth before impact.
