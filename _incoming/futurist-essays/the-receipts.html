THE VAULT

What went into the black books.

1951 TO NOW#01

INVENTION SECRECY ACT

6,471 secrecy orders standing

A 1951 law lets federal agencies seal a patent application and bar the inventor from disclosing it. At the end of fiscal 2024, 6,471 orders were in force. That year added 356, nearly triple the year before.

VERIFY Patent Office figures, hosted by FAS

1971#02

THE SCREENING LIST

20% solar efficiency that triggered review

A 1971 screening list marked solar cells for possible secrecy once they passed 20 percent efficiency, and energy conversion systems past 70 to 80 percent. A 2010 request for the current list was denied.

VERIFY FAS Secrecy News, 2010

1973 TO 1997#03

THE SECRET MATH

24 YRS kept classified

Mathematicians at Britain's GCHQ worked out public-key cryptography in the early 1970s. It is the math that now secures online banking and shopping. Their work stayed classified until December 1997, long after Stanford and MIT researchers reinvented it in public and took the credit.

VERIFY NSA Cryptologic Hall of Honor

JAN 1943#04

TESLA'S PAPERS

2 DAYS from death to seizure

Two days after Nikola Tesla died in a New York hotel, the Office of Alien Property took everything in his rooms. An MIT engineer reviewed the papers for the government and judged them speculative. The FBI's file is public. Read it and judge for yourself.

VERIFY FBI Vault, Nikola Tesla file · PBS on the missing papers

DEPT 02

THE CLEARED FIELD

Competitors managed. Product capped.

1924 TO 1939#05

THE PHOEBUS CARTEL

1,000 HRS agreed bulb life

Osram, Philips, General Electric's overseas arms and other bulb makers agreed to hold household bulbs to 1,000 hours. Bulbs of the day commonly ran 1,500 to 2,000. Members' bulbs were tested, and makers paid fines when theirs lasted too long.

VERIFY IEEE Spectrum, The Great Lightbulb Conspiracy

1949#06

NATIONAL CITY LINES

$5,000 General Motors' fine

A federal jury convicted General Motors, Firestone, Standard Oil of California and others of conspiring to monopolize bus and supply sales to National City Lines, a transit operator they had bankrolled. Corporate fines ran $5,000. Executives paid $1 apiece.

VERIFY Case history on Wikipedia, with court citations

DEPT 03

THE WIRED HOUSE

Infrastructure first. Then the service.

OCT 1994#07

CALEA

BY LAW wiretap-ready networks

Congress required phone carriers to design their equipment and services so that a court-ordered intercept can be switched on. The listening capability ships with the network.

VERIFY Justice Dept. Inspector General report

NOV 2024#08

SALT TYPHOON

MULTIPLE U.S. carriers breached

The FBI and CISA confirmed that hackers tied to China's government broke into multiple telecom companies. They stole call records and copied information tied to court-ordered law enforcement requests. A door built for the warrant holds for exactly as long as its lock.

VERIFY FBI and CISA joint statement, Nov. 13, 2024

1970 TO 2018#09

CRYPTO AG

120+ countries sold rigged machines

A Swiss company sold encryption machines to more than 120 countries. The CIA and West German intelligence had secretly owned it since 1970 and could read the traffic. Reporters exposed it in 2020.

VERIFY Washington Post investigation, ADN reprint

MAY 2023#10

THE DOORBELL

$5.8M Ring's FTC settlement

The FTC charged that Ring gave employees and contractors free access to customers' private camera videos. One employee watched thousands of recordings from at least 81 women. Ring paid $5.8 million and moved on.

VERIFY CBS News on the FTC complaint

JAN 2026#11

THE CAR

3 SEC between data pulls

The FTC finalized an order against General Motors and OnStar for collecting precise location and driving behavior from millions of vehicles, as often as every three seconds, and selling it to firms that feed insurers. The order bars that sharing for five years.

VERIFY Federal Trade Commission announcement

DEPT 04

THE 3 A.M. VISITOR

The machine picks locks. The machine stands watch.

JAN 2024#12

THE FAKE CFO

$25.6M wired on a deepfake call

A finance worker at the engineering firm Arup joined a video call with his CFO and colleagues. Every other face on the call was a deepfake. He sent HK$200 million in 15 transfers.

VERIFY CNN report, carried by ABC17

SEPT 2025#13

THE MACHINE BREAK-IN

80–90% of the campaign run by AI

Anthropic reported that a state-sponsored group turned its Claude Code tool on about 30 targets. By the company's account the AI ran 80 to 90 percent of the campaign, with people stepping in at a few decision points. Outside researchers have questioned that figure.

VERIFY Anthropic incident report

MAY 2026#14

THE MACHINE GUARD

10,000+ serious flaws found in a month

Anthropic and about 50 partners reported that a restricted AI model found more than 10,000 high or critical severity software flaws in its first month of defensive use. Mozilla fixed 271 in one Firefox release.

VERIFY Anthropic, Project Glasswing update

WHAT THE TAPE SAYS
