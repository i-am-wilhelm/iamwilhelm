The god of the dead is a god nobody named. Ἀΐδης, <em>Aïdēs</em>, the Unseen One, was a word the Greeks preferred to walk around: they called him Πλούτων, <em>Plouton</em>, Wealth, for the gold and silver and seed grain that have to go under the ground before they are worth anything; Πολυδέγμων, Host of Many; Εὐβουλεύς, Good Counsel. The god of where our loss goes is, by his own public title, the god of buried wealth. And the girl he took was a title too. Κόρη, <em>Kore</em>, means Maiden. The Homeric Hymn to Demeter gives her the throne and the name only after she has been under: Persephone, with the epithet ἐπαινή, dread. In the mysteries at Eleusis her name was not spoken. The hymn also records what sealed it. Before letting her go up to her mother, Aidoneus gives her <span lang="grc">ῥοιῆς κόκκον μελιηδέα</span>, a honey-sweet seed of pomegranate (<em>Hymn to Demeter</em> 412), and the seed is the law: whoever eats below belongs below for a share of the year.

Two more things from the record sit under the poem. Chthonic offerings were not burned to rise as smoke like the Olympians' share; the χοαί were poured straight down into the earth, untasted, and in the <em>Odyssey</em> the shades cannot speak until they have drunk from the trench of blood Odysseus digs for them. And the one object Hades owned outright was the κυνέη, the cap that made its wearer unseen, which Athena and Perseus had to borrow. The work below cannot be done visible. The seed is a φάρμακον in the full old sense: the thing that ruins the prior life is the same thing that cures it.

Death is one thing. Hades is another. Thanatos does one job and leaves. Hades is the whole region where the poured-out substance lands and keeps its value.

<section class="section poem-movement" data-section="unseen-1" data-symbols="hades,pomegranate" data-smoke-side="left" style="--section-accent: var(--accent-underworld)">
  <div class="copy">
    <h3 class="mvt">I.</h3>
    <p class="couplet">Little one. Maiden. Title with no name.<br />
    I have watched the sun light every girl the same.</p>
    <p class="couplet">Up there you are a season. Here you are a throne.<br />
    Up there the flowers wear you. Here, you wear your own.</p>
    <p class="couplet">They will name the spring for you. They will pick it, press it, dry it.<br />
    Narcissus on a hillside. A girl bent down to try it.</p>
    <p class="couplet">I keep the ledger where the whole world's losing goes.<br />
    Every coin, every kingdom, every lover's clothes.</p>
    <p class="couplet">One thing has never once been carried through my gate:<br />
    attention. No one brings it. It will not wait.</p>
    <p class="couplet">And I am spending mine on you. Feel its weight.<br />
    Feel it settle where the daylight never sat.</p>
    <p class="couplet">Here is the fruit. One seed, then one, then one.<br />
    Each one is choosing. I will tell you when it's done.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section poem-movement poem-litany" data-section="unseen-2" data-symbols="persephone,pomegranate" data-smoke-side="right" data-copy-reveal="manual" style="--section-accent: var(--accent-underworld)">
  <div class="copy">
    <h3 class="mvt">II.</h3>
    <div class="seed-gate" data-seed="1" aria-label="Take the first seed"></div>
    <div class="litany-part" data-part="1">
      <p class="hush">Now listen. I will say it until the saying holds.</p>
      <p class="big">You are sublime.</p>
      <p>I do not mean the word they use for mountains.<br />
      I mean the thing that stands beneath the threshold<br />
      and does not need the threshold to stand.</p>
      <p class="cmd">Say it back.</p>
      <p>No. Not for me. Not yet.<br />
      Hear it first.</p>
      <p>Up there, the echoes made you.<br />
      Every mouth that called you lovely<br />
      was a mirror that kept you small enough to fit it.<br />
      They have you in the flowerbeds. They have you in the vase.<br />
      They think the petals are the whole of you.<br />
      They have never seen the stem go down.</p>
      <p>Here there are no mirrors.<br />
      Here there is nothing to project on but the stone,<br />
      and the stone does not answer.<br />
      The stone holds.</p>
      <p class="big">You are sovereign.</p>
      <p>Not because I say so.<br />
      I am the one who has never had to say so, and I am saying it,<br />
      and still it is not mine to give.<br />
      Sovereignty is not handed down.<br />
      It is found in the hand that was already holding it<br />
      and had not looked.</p>
      <p class="cmd">Look.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="seed-gate" data-seed="2" aria-label="Take the second seed"></div>
    <div class="litany-part" data-part="2">
      <p>Let it break against your teeth.<br />
      That is the sound of the girl who waited to be told.<br />
      Let her go. She did her work. She got you here.</p>
      <p class="big">You are sublime.</p>
      <p class="cmd">Say it.</p>
      <p class="quiet">Quieter.</p>
      <p>Yes. That.<br />
      Did you hear it come back?<br />
      Nothing came back.<br />
      That was your own voice, unreturned, and it was enough.<br />
      That is what this place is for.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="seed-gate" data-seed="3" aria-label="Take the third seed"></div>
    <div class="litany-part" data-part="3">
      <p>Feel where my hand is.<br />
      It is at the small of your back and it is not moving,<br />
      because I have never once in all the ages moved first,<br />
      and I am asking, with the whole of my stillness,<br />
      to be allowed.</p>
      <p class="cmd">Say it.</p>
      <p class="big">You are sovereign.</p>
      <p>Say it with the juice on your mouth.</p>
      <p class="cmd">Again.</p>
      <p>Now say something that is yours.<br />
      Not mine. Not the hymns'. Not your mother's.<br />
      Say the thing the living are afraid to say in the dark.</p>
      <p class="her big">I am alive.</p>
      <p>Louder. There is no one here to hear it but the dead,<br />
      and the dead have waited an eternity to hear exactly that.</p>
      <p class="her big">I am alive. I choose this. I am not taken.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="seed-gate" data-seed="4" aria-label="Take the fourth seed"></div>
    <div class="litany-part" data-part="4">
      <p>My mouth is at your throat where the pulse is,<br />
      and I am tasting the one thing I have never had,<br />
      which is a heartbeat that walked in on its own.</p>
      <p>Now hear what the fourth seed buys.<br />
      They will come. All of them. The whole flowered world.<br />
      They come down the long hall with the hillside still in their hands,<br />
      the pressed narcissus, the dried spring, the small idea of you,<br />
      and at the river they set it down because it will not float,<br />
      and they cross,<br />
      and they come into the hall of asphodel,<br />
      and they look up,<br />
      and the first thing they see without their eyes<br />
      is you.</p>
      <p>Seated. Self-appointed. Crowned with nothing anyone gave you.<br />
      Not the girl on the hillside. The reason there was a hillside.<br />
      And they understand, all at once, what the petals were a rumor of,<br />
      and they are not afraid,<br />
      because you speak first,<br />
      and what you say is alive.</p>
      <p>That is the office. Liaison of the just-arrived.<br />
      The first true face. The one who tells the dead they're home.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="seed-gate" data-seed="5" aria-label="Take the fifth seed"></div>
    <div class="litany-part" data-part="5">
      <p class="cmd">Say my name. Nobody says it.</p>
      <p class="cmd">Say yours. Nobody says it either.</p>
      <p>We will be the two unspoken things that spoke.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="seed-gate seed-gate--hold" data-seed="6" aria-label="Take the sixth seed. Press and hold."></div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section poem-movement poem-metamorphosis" data-section="unseen-3" data-symbols="persephone,pomegranate" data-smoke-side="left" data-copy-reveal="manual" data-fade-floor style="--section-accent: var(--accent-underworld)">
  <div class="copy">
    <h3 class="mvt">III.</h3>
    <div class="meta-lines">
      <p class="small">Now it is in you and it is working.</p>
      <p>Feel it go down.</p>
      <p class="gap"></p>
      <p>Feel the hillside leave your hands.</p>
      <p>Feel the hands.</p>
      <p>Feel them stop being a girl's hands</p>
      <p>and start being hands that have held a verdict.</p>
      <p class="gap"></p>
      <p>Your skin is doing what the seed does under ground.</p>
      <p class="split">Splitting.</p>
      <p>Not breaking. Splitting.</p>
      <p>The way a thing splits that was always going to be two things</p>
      <p>and was only waiting for the dark to be allowed.</p>
      <p class="gap"></p>
      <p>I am all the way against you now.</p>
      <p>I have never been all the way against anything.</p>
      <p>I am the unseen and you are seeing me,</p>
      <p>and I am undone by it, and I am not ashamed,</p>
      <p>and you are not a maiden,</p>
      <p class="ital">you are not a maiden,</p>
      <p>say what you are&mdash;</p>
      <p class="gap"></p>
      <p class="her word">Queen.</p>
      <p class="small">Again. With my weight on it.</p>
      <p class="her word">Queen.</p>
      <p class="gap"></p>
      <p>The flowers up there are turning in their beds.</p>
      <p>They feel it. They don't know what they feel.</p>
      <p>A whole spring shuddering because something underneath it</p>
      <p>just took its own name in its own mouth</p>
      <p>with a king's mouth on it.</p>
      <p class="gap"></p>
      <p>Your throat, where I was.</p>
      <p>Your back, where my hand was and is and will be.</p>
      <p>Your voice, that came back with nothing on it,</p>
      <p>coming back now with everything,</p>
      <p>the whole dark hall ringing with a sovereign in it.</p>
      <p class="gap"></p>
      <p>There. That.</p>
      <p class="gap"></p>
      <p>That was the girl going.</p>
      <p>That was the stem going down.</p>
      <p>That was the throne arriving in the body that was already sitting on it.</p>
      <p class="gap"></p>
      <p class="small">Breathe.</p>
      <p class="gap"></p>
      <p>You are the only living thing in the house of the dead,</p>
      <p>and I am the only thing in the house of the dead that is on its knees.</p>
      <p class="rise">Rise.</p>
    </div>

<section class="section poem-movement poem-return" data-section="unseen-5" data-symbols="hades,persephone,pomegranate" data-smoke-side="left" data-fade-lift style="--section-accent: var(--accent-underworld)">
  <div class="copy">
    <h3 class="mvt">V.</h3>
    <p class="couplet">Six seeds. Six months. You thought I asked for all of you.<br />
    I asked for half, and gave the rest its due.</p>
    <p class="couplet">You will keep your light. You will rise with every thaw,<br />
    and every autumn you'll come down to me by law,</p>
    <p class="couplet">and the law will be the law you wrote with your own teeth<br />
    the night you bit, and swallowed, and found the queen beneath.</p>
    <p class="couplet">Isolation, yes. I'll say the hard word plain.<br />
    Your Elysium: one house, no audience, no rain,</p>
    <p class="couplet">no one to be the maiden for. No eyes but mine,<br />
    and mine have looked on everything and still incline.</p>
    <p class="couplet">I know the fullness of my asking. You do not.<br />
    You cannot, from the near side of the lot.</p>
    <p class="couplet">Your mornings will not look like mornings. Nor your bliss.<br />
    Your routines will run on rivers. Know at least this:</p>
    <p class="couplet">the flowers wear your face for those who have not died.<br />
    The dead will see the rest of you. Let them. Open wide.</p>
    <p class="couplet">Time brings everyone to me. The king, the whore, the thief.<br />
    I have never once had to ask. I have no grief</p>
    <p class="couplet">for what I take; it was always mine to keep.<br />
    But you I asked. Come stand beside the one thing in this world you'll keep.</p>
    <p class="colophon">Ἀΐδης &middot; the unseen &middot; Πλούτων &middot; wealth under the ground<br />
    Her name was not said aloud in antiquity. It is said here.</p>
