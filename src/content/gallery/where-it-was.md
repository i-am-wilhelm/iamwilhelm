Through the Lens, No. 3; the label comes from src/content/writings/where-it-was.md.
Built from the owner's draft in the vault, on the True Amazons scrolly
system: tagged passages, one thing to touch per movement, the source ledger
last. Its symbols (the three-string lyre, the charioteer, the lion and the
beast inside the man) are drawn on the page's own hero; the frame carries
Libra, the balance, for Republic 443d: justice as each part doing its own
work, the three strings bound into one. The plate is the page's night, the
dark sea of the dike panel taken down.
