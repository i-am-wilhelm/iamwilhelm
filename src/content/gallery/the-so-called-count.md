The third Mythmaking piece, after Questions Wearing Teeth: the Comte de
Saint-Germain, who arrived in the record with no birthday and left it under
a burial entry that hedged his name, and everyone who finished his story
for him. One fixed canvas behind nine rooms: an empty gilt frame circled by
his aliases, candles for the people he outlived on paper, the king's flawed
diamond and Casanova's coin in the coals, a tea that spells senna, his age
as each teller gave it, the Eckernförde register, the sightings after 1784,
and a touchstone that sorts nine claims into gold, brass and lead. Sound
stays off until the HUD turns it on.
